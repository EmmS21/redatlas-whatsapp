What this is

This is a demo of using Red Atlas data as a distribution layer for appraisers. Instead of their own app or website, they get property insights through WhatsApp, the channel they already use every day. Red Atlas provides the structured Puerto Rico real estate data. The demo shows how that data reaches an appraiser as a clean, readable answer.

RED Atlas

How it works

An appraiser types an address. It goes through WhatsApp and Meta, the distribution channel. The backend calls Red Atlas for structured property records. Templating shapes that raw data into what appraisers want: comps, price per square foot, and market conditions. The reply comes back on WhatsApp.
